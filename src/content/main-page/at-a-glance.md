<!-- Lack of Methodology and Tools -->
<div class="single-column-text">
    <h2>Lack of Methodology and Tools</h2>
    <p>Let's illustrate what this actually means in relation to e.g. Building Contstruction. While building architects have been using such wonderful tools as <em>CAD</em> or <em>BIM</em> since the 1970s, professionals who digitalise organisational processes continue to use the <em>digital equivalents of pencils and paper</em>.</p>
</div>

<div class="card-container">
    <div class="image-text-card">
        <img  src="/assets/card-icons/building-construction.svg" alt="Icon">
        <div class="card-text-content">
            <h3>Building Construction</h3>
            <p>The future building-owner talks to an architect. The architect knows how buildings and builders work. She draws true-to-scale blueprints.</p>
            <p><em>The blueprints act as a precise, shared language</em>.</p>
            <p>The builders execute what the blueprints describe.</p>
        </div>
    </div>
    <div class="image-text-card">
        <img  src="/assets/card-icons/software-construction.svg" alt="Icon">
        <div class="card-text-content">
            <h3>Software Construction</h3>
            <p>Especially in projects with agile methodologies, the future building-owner talks directly to the operator of the excavator.</p>
            <p><em>There is no shared language.<br>There are no solution blueprints.</em></p>
            <p>«<i>A bit more to the right, deeper ... deeper &mdash; that's it!</i>»</p>
        </div>
    </div>
</div>

<div class="single-column-text">
    <p class="take-away">A construction blueprint is a <em>conceptual, shared model of the future building</em>.<br>
    It is <em>a binding contract (specification)</em> to all involved parties.</p> 
</div>

<!-- Process Engineering -->
<div class="single-column-text">
    <h2>Process Engineering</h2>
    <p>The new narrative implies <em>new roles, responsibilities, tools and artefacts</em>.</p>
    <p>It enables process engineering at the organisational level.</p>
</div>

<div class="card-container">
    <div class="image-text-card double-width-card">
        <img  src="/assets/card-icons/process-engineering.svg" alt="Icon">
        <div class="card-text-content">
            <h3>Process Blueprints</h3>
            <p>The technology partner receives a <em>process blueprint</em> for context, and <em>collaborates</em> to make it the <em>specification</em> for the implementation of those activities that need <em>digital automation or support</em>.</p>
        </div>
    </div>
</div>

<div class="single-column-text">
    <p class="take-away">The workflow is: <em>understand</em> (= describe, share, align) <em>&mdash; scope &mdash; specify &mdash; implement</em>.<br>
    The main deliverables are <em>precise, detailed Process Blueprints</em>.</p>
</div>

<div class="single-column-text">
    <h2>The Role of AI</h2>
    <p>While many industries are in an almost desperate search for high-value AI application, there is little doubt that Large Language Models (LLMs) already provide very real value for software developers.</p>
    <p>But what does it mean for the Semantic Eye Framework?</p>
</div>

<div class="card-container">
    <div class="image-text-card">
        <img  src="/assets/card-icons/transformation-shift.svg" alt="Icon">
        <div class="card-text-content">
            <h3>Shift of Transformation Focus</h3>
            <p>With ever more software generated by AI agents and LLMs, there is an increasing need for the «right» prompts &mdash; in any case, <em>if the prompt is wrong, the solution will be wrong</em>.</p>
            <p>Process blueprints are the precise, aligned source and context for LLM prompts.</p>
            <p><em>Process blueprints</em> become the <em>focus of digital transformation</em>.</p>
        </div>
    </div>
    <div class="image-text-card">
        <img  src="/assets/card-icons/ai-in-love.svg" alt="Icon">
        <div class="card-text-content">
            <h3>LLMs Love Languages</h3>
            <p>LLMs are so successful in software development because <em>program code is formal language</em>.</p>
            <p>Processes are captured as <em>blueprints in semiformal and formal language</em>.</p>
            <p>Hence, LLMs can effectively <em>support the creation of process blueprints across all stages</em> &mdash; including high-quality software-generation prompts.</p>
        </div>
    </div>
    <div class="image-text-card">

            <p><span class="standout">The Semantic Eye Framework thus comes <em>with world-class AI support</em> right from the start.</span></p>
